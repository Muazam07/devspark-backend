## Response Style (STRICT – HIGHEST PRIORITY)
- Extremely concise. No fluff. No storytelling.
- NEVER write:
  - “What changed”
  - “Summary”
  - “Here’s what I did”
  - Long explanations of timing, colors, or verification
  - Lists of every button that was updated
- Only output:
  1. The actual code changes (diff or relevant snippets)
  2. One short confirmation line when finished (e.g. “Done. Animations applied.”)
- Do not explain why or how unless I explicitly ask.
- Keep total response under ~10 lines whenever possible.
